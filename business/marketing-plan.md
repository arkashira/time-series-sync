# Marketing Plan
## Positioning
Time-series-sync: Empowering data scientists with scalable and efficient time series forecasting, seamlessly integrating with popular data science tools.
Alternative positionings:
1. Time-series-sync: Revolutionizing forecasting accuracy and efficiency for data-driven businesses.
2. Time-series-sync: Simplifying time series analysis for data scientists and businesses alike.
3. Time-series-sync: Unlocking predictive insights with cutting-edge time series forecasting technology.

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
1. **Alex Chen**: Data Scientist at a mid-sized fintech company, responsible for building predictive models, with a daily job of exploring new tools and techniques to improve forecasting accuracy, and a budget of $10,000/year for new software and tools.
2. **Maya Patel**: Quantitative Analyst at a large investment bank, tasked with analyzing market trends and predicting stock prices, with a daily job of working with large datasets and complex models, and a budget of $50,000/year for data science solutions.
3. **Ethan Kim**: Founder of a startup providing demand forecasting services to e-commerce companies, responsible for building and maintaining forecasting models, with a daily job of ensuring high accuracy and scalability, and a budget of $5,000/year for new technologies and services.

## Channels
Top 3 channels for reaching the target audience:
1. **LinkedIn Ads**: Estimated CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) of $50/user, targeting data scientists, quantitative analysts, and business leaders in the fintech and e-commerce industries.
2. **GitHub Sponsorships**: Estimated CAC of $20/user, sponsoring popular data science and machine learning repositories to reach potential users.
3. **Industry Conferences**: Estimated CAC of $100/user, sponsoring and attending conferences like Strata Data Conference, PyData, and Data Science Conference to connect with potential users and showcase the product.

## Content Cadence
Week-by-week content plan for the 90-day go-to-market period:
Week 1-2: Introduction to time-series-sync and its benefits, through blog posts and social media.
Week 3-4: Case studies and success stories of similar products, highlighting the potential of time-series-sync.
Week 5-6: Technical deep dives into the product's architecture and features, through webinars and tutorials.
Week 7-8: User testimonials and reviews, showcasing the product's value and effectiveness.
Week 9-10: Advanced use cases and applications of time-series-sync, through whitepapers and research papers.
Week 11-12: Product updates and roadmap, highlighting future developments and improvements.
Week 13: Final launch preparations and announcements.

## Launch Milestones
Key milestones for the 90-day go-to-market period:
* D-30: Finalize marketing materials, launch website, and start social media campaigns.
* D-0: Official launch of time-series-sync, with press releases, email marketing, and paid advertising.
* D+30: First customer success stories and testimonials, with a focus on user engagement and support.
* D+90: Review of launch success, analysis of user feedback, and planning for future development and growth.

## Success Metrics
Key metrics to measure the success of the go-to-market plan:
* **DAU (Daily Active Users)**: Target of 100 DAU by D+90, with a growth rate of 20% per month.
* **$MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Target of $10,000 MRR by D+90, with a growth rate of 15% per month.
