 # Partner Integration Roadmap for Time-Series-Sync

The following is a proposed partner integration roadmap for the Time-Series-Sync product. The goal is to integrate with popular SaaS/APIs to enhance the product's functionality, provide a seamless user experience, and increase its value proposition.

## 1. Google BigQuery (Integration Effort: Large, Value-Add: High)
- Free-tier limit: 1 TB per month
- Integration: Enable users to directly import and analyze time series data from Google BigQuery within Time-Series-Sync for efficient forecasting.
- Rationale: Google BigQuery is a powerful and scalable data warehouse that can handle large volumes of time series data. Integrating with BigQuery will provide users with a seamless data import and analysis experience.

## 2. AWS QuickSight (Integration Effort: Large, Value-Add: High)
- Free-tier limit: 5 GB per month
- Integration: Allow users to visualize time series data and forecasts within AWS QuickSight for better insights and data-driven decision making.
- Rationale: AWS QuickSight is a fast, cloud-powered business intelligence service that makes it easy to visualize and analyze data. Integrating with QuickSight will provide users with powerful data visualization capabilities.

## 3. Microsoft Power BI (Integration Effort: Large, Value-Add: High)
- Free-tier limit: 1 GB per user per month
- Integration: Enable users to visualize time series data and forecasts within Microsoft Power BI for better insights and data-driven decision making.
- Rationale: Microsoft Power BI is a comprehensive business analytics service that delivers insights throughout your organization. Integrating with Power BI will provide users with powerful data visualization capabilities.

## 4. InfluxData InfluxDB (Integration Effort: Medium, Value-Add: High)
- Free-tier limit: 1 retention year, 16 MB/s write, 16 MB/s read
- Integration: Allow users to store and query time series data within InfluxDB for efficient forecasting and analysis.
- Rationale: InfluxDB is an open-source time series database built for fast, high-availability storage and retrieval of time series data. Integrating with InfluxDB will provide users with a scalable and efficient data storage solution.

## 5. Algolia (Integration Effort: Medium, Value-Add: Medium)
- Free-tier limit: 100,000 operations per month
- Integration: Enable users to search and filter time series data within Algolia for faster data retrieval and analysis.
- Rationale: Algolia is a powerful search API that provides fast and relevant search results. Integrating with Algolia will provide users with a fast and efficient way to search and filter their time series data.

## 6. Segment (Integration Effort: Medium, Value-Add: Medium)
- Free-tier limit: 100,000 tracked users per month
- Integration: Allow users to send time series data to Segment for analysis and marketing automation.
- Rationale: Segment is a customer data infrastructure that collects, unifies, and sends customer data to any destination. Integrating with Segment will provide users with a way to analyze their time series data and automate their marketing efforts.

## 7. Zapier (Integration Effort: Low, Value-Add: Medium)
- Free-tier limit: 5 Zaps, 100 tasks per month
- Integration: Enable users to automate workflows between Time-Series-Sync and other popular apps using Zapier.
- Rationale: Zapier is a powerful automation tool that connects apps and services together. Integrating with Zapier will provide users with a way to automate their workflows and save time.

These integrations will not only enhance the functionality of Time-Series-Sync but also provide users with a more comprehensive and valuable solution for their time series forecasting needs. Additionally, integrating with services that offer affiliate/revenue-share programs will help increase the revenue potential of the product.
